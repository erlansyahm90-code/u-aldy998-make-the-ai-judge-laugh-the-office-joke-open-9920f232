## The joke

> My AI assistant now answers my emails, attends my meetings, and just got asked to write my performance review — about me.

A 3-beat office joke with a reversal: the AI assistant takes over more and
more of its owner's job — emails, then meetings — until the tool turns on
its owner and is asked to judge the owner itself.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

1–200 character gate).
